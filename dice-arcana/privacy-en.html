Dice Arcana Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Controller

Beñat González Laso (Spain), developer of the Dice Arcana app ("the app").
Contact: benat.gonzalez.dev@gmail.com

2. Summary

  • The app has no user accounts and does not ask for your email or other personal data. You are identified by a random player code created by the app itself (shown in Settings).
  • Your progress and settings are stored on your device.
  • For the daily challenge ranking, your scores for that challenge are uploaded with your code, your player name and your in-game avatar (section 5).
  • To fix bugs and improve the game, crash reports and, if you consent, usage statistics are sent (section 6).
  • The app shows Google AdMob ads. Google processes certain device data to serve and measure them. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first.

3. Data stored on your device

Your progress (level, unlocks, saved runs, statistics) and settings (sound, reminders…) are stored in the app's internal storage on your device. Except as described in sections 5 and 6, they are not sent to any server. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob, by Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC, to show ads. These include optional ads you can watch for an in-game reward. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • the device advertising ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device information (model, operating system, language) and app information;
  • your interaction with ads and SDK diagnostic data.

Consent. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form (an IAB-certified consent management platform) is shown when you open the app. You can accept, decline or choose options. If you decline, non-personalised or limited ads may be shown. You can change your choice at any time in Ajustes → Privacidad de anuncios (Settings → Ad privacy).

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). For what is strictly necessary to serve non-personalised ads and prevent fraud, the basis is legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)). Google processes and retains this data under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google → Ads).

5. Daily challenge ranking (Google Firebase)

When you finish a daily challenge attempt, the app uploads to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), using an anonymous Firebase Authentication sign-in that asks for no personal data:

  • your player code, your player name and the avatar (character) you have chosen;
  • your best score of that day, the score of each attempt, which attempt it was, and whether you completed the challenge;
  • the date and time of the upload.

Other players see your position, name, avatar, code and score in the ranking. The purpose is to show the ranking; the legal basis is the performance of the service you request by playing the challenge (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Scores are kept while the ranking exists or until you ask for them to be deleted (section 12). Days played with test tools are not uploaded.

The app also reads from Firebase, without sending your data, the minimum game version (to tell you if you need to update).

6. Crash reports and statistics (Google Firebase)

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). If the app crashes or fails, a technical report is sent: what failed, the device model, the Android and app versions, and your player code so that several crashes from the same device can be linked. The legal basis is legitimate interest in the app working properly (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase). Only if you give consent in the form described in section 4, usage data is sent: which parts of the game are used (for example, starting or finishing a run or watching an ad), device data and your player code. It helps us know what to improve. You can withdraw consent in Ajustes → Privacidad de anuncios (Settings → Ad privacy).

Google keeps this data according to its own periods (Crashlytics, about 90 days; Analytics, according to the project settings: 2 months by default and 14 months at most). More information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

7. Game time

So that the daily challenge is the same for everyone, the app asks a Google server for the current time with a minimal request. We send no identifier or personal data. Google receives the technical data inherent to any connection, such as your IP address.

8. Notifications

If you turn them on, the app schedules a daily challenge reminder on your own device. These are local notifications: no server is used and no data is sent. You can turn them off in Settings or in Android settings.

9. Game engine (Unity)

The app is built with Unity. The engine may send Unity Technologies anonymous technical data about the device (for example, its model and OS version) to improve its software. More information: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

10. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children. In the European Economic Area, if you are under the minimum age of digital consent in your country, ask a parent or guardian to manage ad consent for you.

11. International transfers

Google (AdMob and Firebase) may process data outside the European Economic Area under the safeguards provided by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework).

12. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, and withdraw your consent, by writing to benat.gonzalez.dev@gmail.com. Include your player code (Settings) so we can find and delete your scores and reports. For advertising data processed by Google you can also use its tools: myaccount.google.com. You may lodge a complaint with your data protection authority; in Spain, the AEPD (www.aepd.es).

13. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with its date. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
